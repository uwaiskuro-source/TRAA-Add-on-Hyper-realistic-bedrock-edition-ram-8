​Lightning will have an effect on clouds, making them shine/glow if the lightning travels from inside the cloud to the ground, plus other possible effects. As for phenomena like a "green sky," the sky itself turns greenish-blue, but the clouds don't. However, the clouds will look greenish-blue because of the sky's reflection, and I will definitely add SFX for this.
​For the mod's realistic features, only clearly visible effects will be included (very clear, clear, slightly clear, a little bit clear). If an effect isn't very clear or noticeable, it won't be included to prevent lag caused by unnecessary features.
​All atmospheric phenomena studied by humans will be included—not just clouds, but many sky/atmospheric phenomena.
​Cloud movement won't use a "delete from the back and spawn new particles in front" method. Instead, clouds are non-killable, non-hittable entities that can only move. Cloud particles smoothly follow the entity, like a repeating teleport, so movement looks seamless.
​All tornado shapes will be included, along with their movement, pathing, appearance, and swaying behavior (like a rope tornado twisting or "dancing") based on conditions. Exotic atmospheric phenomena like firenados, sharknados, Backrooms nados (where getting lifted vibrates your atoms and noclips you to the Backrooms), and every cyclone category will be in the game.

​Tornado Shapes and Types:
​1. Main Tornado Shapes (Real Visual Categories)
​Rope Tornado: Very thin, winding funnel resembling a rope. Typically appears when a tornado weakens or enters its final phase.
​Cone Tornado: Classic cone shape, wide at the cloud base and narrowing sharply toward the ground.
​Stovepipe Tornado: Relatively uniform width from top to bottom, looking like a pipe or chimney.
​Wedge Tornado: Monster tornado that is extremely wide, looking like a massive wall of dark clouds touching the ground.
​Bowl-Shaped Tornado: A visual description of a funnel that bulges in specific areas, resembling a bowl.
​Elephant-Trunk Tornado: Long, thick, curved funnel hanging from the sky like an elephant's trunk.
​Hourglass Tornado: Shaped like an hourglass, narrower in the middle than at the top and bottom.
​Drill-Bit / Spiral-Like Tornado: A sharp, spiraling funnel resembling a massive drill bit.
​Dead-Man-Walking: Legendary informal name for a massive tornado with multiple subvortices at its base, making it look like a walking giant from a distance.
​Irregular / Complex Tornado: Random, asymmetric vortex that continuously changes shape as the storm evolves.
​2. Multi-Vortex Tornado Structures
​Multi-Vortex Tornado: One large parent circulation containing several smaller sub-vortices orbiting together inside it.
​Twin Tornadoes: Two separate tornadoes produced simultaneously by the same supercell storm ecosystem.
​Satellite Tornado: A much smaller secondary tornado orbiting around a larger main tornado.
​3. Other Conditional Visuals
​Cylinder: Straight vertical cylinder shape (similar to a stovepipe).
​Wide Irregular Tornado: Very wide vortex with asymmetric wall contours.
​Narrowing / Rope-Out: The narrowing phase as the tornado loses energy before dissipating.
​Rain-Wrapped Tornado: Dangerous tornado completely hidden behind a rain shaft, making it invisible from the outside.
​Invisible-Looking Tornado: The ground-level circulation is active, but the upper condensation funnel isn't visible yet due to air parameters.
​4. Tornado-Like Phenomena (Non-Supercell)
​Landspout: A narrow funnel formed from the ground up through non-supercell mechanisms, usually picking up ground dust.
​Waterspout: Intense spinning column of air occurring over water (seas or lakes).
​Dust Devil: Rotating dust column caused by extreme surface heating on clear, hot days (not a meteorological storm).
​5. Special TRAA Game & Exotic Fiction Variants
​Sharknado
​Undetected Tornado: Cannot be detected by radar.
​Eclipse Tornado: Pitch-black tornado that does not absorb light.
​Poisoning Tornado

​Blinding Tornado: Causes blindness.
​Sonic Boom Tornado: Causes hearing loss and features Warden-like sonic booms.
​Water Tornado: Formed from high humidity, turning into actual liquid water.
​Lava Tornado: Extreme heat and flying rocks melt terrain into lava.
​Crash Tornado: Instantly crashes your game if you get lifted (doesn't affect unlifted players).
​Radiation Tornado: Radioactive vortex.
​Backrooms Tornado (Variant): Player's atoms vibrate intensely upon lift, showing the Backrooms for 1 second before screen turns black, killing the player and sending them back to spawn.
​Tornado from the Sky?: A parentless tornado with an eye resembling a typhoon, occurring when the upper sky lacks moisture, but ground-level air is humid enough to form a funnel.
​Unbelievable Size Tornado: Slow-moving, unpredictable monster tornado measuring 3–5 miles wide.
